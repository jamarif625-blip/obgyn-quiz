How XP is earned
Correct answer +10 XP · quiz completed +25 XP · first question in a topic you have never answered +25 XP · first study session of the day +50 XP · daily challenge completed +40 XP.

Combo multiplier — consecutive correct answers in one session: 3 in a row ×1.2, 5 ×1.5, 10 ×2. A wrong answer resets the combo for that session only; your total XP and mastery are never reduced.

Levels — 0 Beginner · 1,000 Learner · 3,000 Scholar · 8,000 Clinician · 15,000 Expert.

Mastery — 🟡 on track at 70%, 🟢 mastered at 85% over at least 8 answers, 🏆 sustained mastery at 90% held on three separate study days. Reaching 95% in a topic also wins its badge.

Streak freezes — you start with 3. Miss one day and a freeze is spent automatically instead of losing the streak, so a single busy day is not devastating.
